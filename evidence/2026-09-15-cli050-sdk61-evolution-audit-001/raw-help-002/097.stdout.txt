Description:
Capture a screen recording from the preferred current device

Usage:
  $ pico-cli capture record

Options:
  -d, --device <device>  Target device id
  -o, --out <path>       Local output path
  -t, --time <seconds>   Recording duration seconds (1-1800; longer requests are clamped to 1800)
                         (default: 15)
  -h, --help             Display this message
