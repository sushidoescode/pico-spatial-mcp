Usage:
  pico-cli device <command> [options]

Commands:
  battery               Show battery information for the preferred current device
  connect <address>     Connect to a device over WiFi
  disconnect [address]  Disconnect from a device
  info                  Show information about the preferred current device
  list                  List connected devices
  props                 Show system properties for the preferred current device
  shell <cmd>           Execute a raw ADB shell command

Options:
  -h, --help  Show this message and exit.
